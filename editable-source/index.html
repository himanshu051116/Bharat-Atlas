Map source and limitations

Our political geography should eventually follow Survey of India's 2026 reference, including the entire extent claimed by India. Some areas are administered by Pakistan or China; a source-aware atlas must distinguish claims from actual administration.

The public release has NO certified national or state political boundaries. Physical coastline geometry is not a territorial claim. See the official Survey of India 2026 political map.

City-centre points are approximate demonstrations, not a national gazetteer. The check markers are approximate sample points. They cannot detect the exact alignment of a frontier, polygon overlap or every missing area. An official-data licence, polygon-by-polygon GIS comparison and independent review remain mandatory before a validated release.

To view regional boundaries: download appropriately licensed, source-verified administrative polygons, convert them to GeoJSON with properties.name and Polygon/MultiPolygon geometry, and use Import GeoJSON. Imported data is local to your browser and not automatically certified. See Survey of India administrative boundary downloads and the source's terms before redistribution.
